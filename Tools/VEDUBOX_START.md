# VEDUBOX START

## Product Specification & Architecture Document

---

# PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Vedubox Start is a digital academy platform designed specifically for SMEs.

It enables companies to launch employee training programs without requiring:

* a training department
* LMS expertise
* technical knowledge

The platform combines ready-made training content with simple employee training management.

The core promise:

"Create your company academy in 5 minutes."

---

# PRODUCT POSITIONING

## Main Positioning

Ready-made digital academy for SMEs.

## Value Proposition

Digitize employee training in minutes.

## Elevator Pitch

Vedubox Start helps SMEs create, assign and track employee training without needing a learning management specialist.

---

# TARGET AUDIENCE

Primary Users:

* Company Owners
* HR Managers
* Operations Managers
* Office Managers

Company Size:

* 5–250 Employees

User Characteristics:

* Non-technical
* Time-constrained
* No LMS experience
* Wants fast implementation

---

# PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

The user does NOT want to manage an LMS.

The user wants to:

* onboard employees
* assign training
* ensure compliance
* track completion

The product must remove complexity.

The user experience must feel:

* simple
* fast
* friendly
* modern

---
